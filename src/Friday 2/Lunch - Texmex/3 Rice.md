## Ingredients

| Ingredient       | Catering | Home | Prep |
|------------------|----------|------|------|
| Rice, Long Grain | 40 kg    |      |      |
| Bay Leaves       | 50 g     |      |      |

## Method

1. Rinse the rice.
2. Cook with bay leaves in the rice cooker.
3. Fluff and hold warm.
