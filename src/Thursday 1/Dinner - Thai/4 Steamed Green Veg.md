# Steamed Green Veg
Leafy greens sliced into ribbons and steamed briefly.

| Field  | Value      |
|--------|------------|
| Type   | side       |
| Vessel | 40.0       |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient   | Catering | Home | Prep                     |
|--------------|----------|------|--------------------------|
| Leafy Greens | 8 kg     |      | Wash, slice into ribbons |

## Method

1. Wash and slice the greens into ribbons.
2. Steam briefly — keep bright and just tender.
