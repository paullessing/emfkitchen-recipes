## Method

1. Make the marinade: yoghurt, lemon, turmeric, chilli, cumin, garam masala, garlic and ginger.
2. Marinate the chicken overnight.
3. Cook the chicken (fry or oven), reserving the juices.
4. Fry onions until soft; add ginger, garlic, garam masala, cumin and chilli until fragrant.
5. Add tomato purée and cook until darkened; add the tomatoes and simmer.
6. Blend smooth; stir in the cream and fenugreek (kasoori methi); taste.
7. Add drained chickpeas to the base for the veg option; stir the chicken into the meat portions.

## Notes

Sauce technique from Delish (butter chickpeas) & RecipeTin Eats (butter chicken) — PDFs in the project folder. **Baking soda** helps soften the chickpeas; darken the tomato purée well before adding tomatoes.
