# Beef Burgers
Frozen beef burgers, BBQ-glazed and baked.

| Field | Value         |
|-------|---------------|
| Type  | meat          |

| Diet  | Contains beef |

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Catering | Home | Prep              |
|------------|----------|------|-------------------|
| Burgers    | 90 ea    |      | Defrost in fridge |

## Method

1. Take the burgers from the fridge.
2. Brush with BBQ sauce.
3. Bake until cooked through.
4. (Optional: slow-bake a batch after lunch service.)
