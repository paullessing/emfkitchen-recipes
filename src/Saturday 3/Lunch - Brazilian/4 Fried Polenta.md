# Fried Polenta
Set polenta cut and fried crisp.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Catering | Home | Prep |
|------------|----------|------|------|
| Polenta    | 10 kg    |      |      |

## Method

1. Cook the polenta and season with salt, pepper and engevita.
2. Spread into oiled gastros and chill until firm.
3. Turn out, slice, and fry until crisp and golden.
