# Brazilian Cottage Pie
Vegetable and soy-protein filling under a butternut-and-potato mash, topped with breadcrumbs and baked. Also called <i>Vegetable Escondidinho</i>.

## Method

1. Roast the squash and boil the potatoes; mash together with stock and season well.
2. Sweat and cook down the filling: onion, garlic, carrot, pepper, courgette, mushroom, sweetcorn, soy protein, tomato and spices. Finish with lime and parsley.
3. Top the filling with the mash, then breadcrumbs.
4. Bake until golden.
