# Tofu Feijoada
Vegan feijoada: spiced fried jackfruit crushed for a pulled texture, in a smoky black-bean stew thickened by mashing some of the beans.

## Method

1. Dust the jackfruit with cumin and paprika and fry in plenty of oil until browned; set aside.
2. Fry the onions until soft; add garlic and smoked paprika.
3. Add the peppers and fry.
4. Return the jackfruit and crush with a potato masher to get a fibrous, pulled texture.
5. Add bay leaves and stock; simmer to meld.
6. Drain the black beans (keep the liquid); mash about a third, add all the beans and simmer to thicken.
7. Season with liquid smoke, salt and lime; loosen with the bean liquid if needed.
