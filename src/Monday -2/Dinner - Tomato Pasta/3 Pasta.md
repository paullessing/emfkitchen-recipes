| Ingredient              | Catering | Home | Prep |

| Pasta, Penne or Fussili | 30 kg    |      |      |

### Gluten Free

| Ingredient              | Catering | Home | Prep |
|-------------------------|----------|------|------|
| Pasta, Gluten Free      | 2 kg     |      |      |

## Method

1. Boil the pasta in well-salted water until al dente.
2. Cook the gluten-free pasta in a separate pan.
3. Drain and toss with a little oil to stop it sticking.
