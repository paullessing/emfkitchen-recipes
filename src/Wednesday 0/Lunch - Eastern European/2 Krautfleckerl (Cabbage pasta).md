# Krautfleckerl (Cabbage pasta)
Austrian cabbage-and-pasta dish: onions and cabbage cooked down, tossed with pasta and caraway. Bacon goes into the non-veg portions only.

## Ingredients

| Ingredient                     | Catering | Home | Prep                   |
|--------------------------------|----------|------|------------------------|
| Pasta (Flat e.g. Farfalle)     | 20 kg    |      | Pre cooked             |
| Cabbage eg Savoy or sweetheart | 40 kg    |      | 1inch squares          |
| Onions                         | 10 kg    |      | chopped, medium        |
| Caraway seeds                  | 250 g    |      |                        |
| Parsley fresh                  | 700 g    |      | Chopped small          |
| White wine vinegar             | 1000 ml  |      |                        |

### Bacon
| Ingredient                     | Catering | Home | Prep                   |
|--------------------------------|----------|------|------------------------|
| Bacon                          | 8 kg     |      | cubed or small squares |

## Method

1. Boil the pasta.
2. Fry the bacon in a separate pan.
3. Fry onions until soft, add cabbage and cook until soft.
4. Deglaze with white wine vinegar; add the pasta and caraway to taste.
5. Stir bacon into the non-veg portions; finish with parsley.
