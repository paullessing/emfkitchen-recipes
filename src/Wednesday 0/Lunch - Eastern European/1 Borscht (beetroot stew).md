# Borscht (beetroot stew)
Beetroot and vegetable stew with red lentils, built by frying the vegetables in stages, simmered in stock and finished with dill; served with sour cream.

## Method

1. Wash the red lentils until the water runs clear.
2. Fry carrots and celeriac until soft and browned.
3. Add onions, leeks and paprika (smoked + hot); fry until golden.
4. Add cabbage and beetroot; fry to soften.
5. Add stock and potatoes.
6. Simmer until potatoes are almost done.
7. Add lentils and dill; simmer to thicken.
8. Serve with sour cream (dairy + vegan) on the side.
