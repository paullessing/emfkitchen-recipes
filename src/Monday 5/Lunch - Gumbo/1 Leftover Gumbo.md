# Leftover Gumbo
End-of-week gumbo built from the week's leftover proteins and vegetables in a roux-thickened stock, over rice.

## Method

1. Make a roux (fat + flour) cooked to a deep brown.
2. Add onion, pepper and celery if available; cook down.
3. Add leftover proteins and vegetables.
4. Add stock and simmer to a thick, savoury gumbo.
5. Season; serve over rice.

## Notes

Flexible — built from whatever is left. Keep quantities to taste.
