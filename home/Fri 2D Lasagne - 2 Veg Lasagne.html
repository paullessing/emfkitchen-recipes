Veg Lasagne

Serves 210p 14 traysVessel kipperDiet Vegetarian
Recipe overview
Lentil-and-vegetable ragù layered with pasta, béchamel and mozzarella, baked in two stages.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Onions 15 kg small cubes
Tinned tomatoes 24 kg —
Lasagne sheets (vg, dry) 10 kg —
Aubergine 9 kg thin slices
Courgette 9 kg thin slices
Red lentils 11 kg washed
Carrots 9 kg small cubes or thin slices
Dried Basil 0.2 kg —
Oregano 75 g —
Balsamic Vinegar 1 L —
Garlic 2 kg —
Mozzarella, grated or pizza 10 kg Sliced or grated
Mozzarella, vegan 2 kg —

Method

  1. Fry onions, garlic and dried herbs until golden.
  2. Add carrots and fry until soft.
  3. Deglaze with balsamic.
  4. Add tinned tomatoes and washed red lentils.
  5. Simmer to a soft ragù.
  6. Layer: ragù, veg slices (aubergine/courgette), pasta, béchamel; repeat.
  7. Bake ~220°C for 30–45 min, then cool overnight.
  8. Top with mozzarella (dairy + a vegan batch) and bake ~230°C for 10 min until browned.
