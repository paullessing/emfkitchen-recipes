Chilli con carne

Serves 70Vessel Big potDiet Contains beef
Recipe overview
Beef mince chilli with kidney beans; fresh chillies served on the side.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Onions 2.5 kg Diced 1inch
Kidney beans 4.5 kg Drained
Tinned Tomatoes 4.5 kg —
Beef Mince 9 kg —
Red Peppers 3 kg Diced 1inch
Cumin 0.1 kg —
Smoked Paprika 0.1 kg —
Chilli Powder 0.1 kg —
Cayenne Pepper 0.1 kg —
Tomato Puree 1 kg —
Red Chillies 500 g —

Method

  1. Fry onions with the spices (cumin, smoked paprika, chilli, cayenne) until golden.
  2. Add the beef mince and spices, with a little bicarbonate of soda to brown it faster.
  3. Fry until well browned.
  4. Deglaze with stock.
  5. Add kidney beans and tomatoes.
  6. Simmer down; loosen with stock if dry. Serve red chillies on the side.
