Hummus

Serves 130Vessel 13kgDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
Chickpea hummus with tahini, lemon, roasted & raw garlic and cumin.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Tinned Chickpeas 20 kg Cooked again until really tender
Tahini 2.4 kg —
Lemons, juice of 52 ea —
Garlic 40 ea —
Garlic Roasted 40 ea —
Cumin 320 g —
Salt 320 g —
Olive Oil 2 l —

Method

  1. Whisk the tahini and lemon juice together until pale and fluffy (big whisk or blender).
  2. Add the garlic, chickpeas, cumin and olive oil, and blend smooth.
  3. Season with salt (more than you'd think), loosening with more oil if needed.
