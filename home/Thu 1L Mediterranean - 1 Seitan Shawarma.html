Seitan Shawarma

Serves 200Vessel kippfanneDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
Home-made seitan from vital wheat gluten and shawarma spices, cooked, shredded and fried crisp, served pitta/kebab style.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Wheat Gluten 14 kg —
Smoked Paprika 300 g —
Paprika 300 g —
Garlic powder 300 g —
Cumin 300 g —
Salt 400 g —
Pepper 400 g —
Oil 2 L —
Ras el hanout 300 g —
Vegetable Stock existing l —

Method

  1. Mix the wheat gluten with the spices (smoked paprika, paprika, garlic powder, cumin, ras el hanout, salt, pepper) and vegetable stock to a stiff dough; knead.
  2. Cook the seitan (steam/simmer/bake) until firm, then cool.
  3. Shred on the veg-prep machine (biggest attachment).
  4. Fry the shredded seitan in oil until crisp and browned.
  5. Season and pack pitta / kebab style.
