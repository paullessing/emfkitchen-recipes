Tarka Dal

Vessel 150.0Diet Vegan
Recipe overview
Chickpea-and-red-lentil dal finished with a bloomed-spice tarka.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Dried Chickpeas 18 kg wash before use
Red Lentils 18 kg wash before use
Fresh Tomatoes 10 kg de seeded, de juiced, cubed, save juice
Onions 24 kg medium cubes
Green chillies 1.2 kg de seeded and cut up
Red chillies 1.2 kg de seeded and cut up
Oil 1.5 l —
Garam Masala 0.3 kg —
Fennel Seeds 0.15 kg —
Cumin seeds 0.15 kg —
Garlic 2.4 kg half roasted
Turmeric 0.15 kg —
Coriander seeds 0.3 kg —
Fresh Coriander 500 g —

Method

  1. Wash the lentils and chickpeas until the water runs clear.
  2. Simmer lentils with turmeric and chillies until soft.
  3. In a separate pan, bloom cumin, fennel and coriander seeds with dried chilli in oil.
  4. Add onion and garlic; fry until translucent.
  5. Add tomatoes and garam masala; cook down.
  6. Stir the tarka into the lentils; finish with fresh coriander.
