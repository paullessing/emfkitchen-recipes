Butter Beans in Tomato Sauce

Serves 260Vessel topfDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
Greek-style gigantes: butter beans simmered in a smoky tomato-and-oregano sauce.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Butter Beans, Dry 10 kg Soak overnight (or use tinned)
Tinned Tomatoes 30 kg —
Onions 8 kg small cubes
Smoked Paprika 300 g —
Oregano 80 g —

Method

  1. Fry the onions with smoked paprika and oregano.
  2. Deglaze with the tinned tomatoes and simmer to thicken.
  3. Fold in the butter beans; season and simmer to meld.
Dried butter beans need soaking & pre-cooking (10 kg dry ≈ 20 kg cooked) — or use tinned.
