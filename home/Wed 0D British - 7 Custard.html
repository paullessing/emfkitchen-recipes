Custard

Serves 260Vessel KipperDiet Vegetarian
Recipe overview
Pouring custard from custard powder, milk and sugar, with a separate oat-milk batch.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Milk, UHT 50 l —
GF Oat Milk 10 l —
Sugar 3.5 kg —
Custard Powder 3.5 kg —

Method

  1. Whisk custard powder and sugar with a little cold milk to a smooth paste.
  2. Heat the rest of the milk, whisk in the paste and cook until thickened.
  3. Make a separate GF oat-milk batch — label it.
