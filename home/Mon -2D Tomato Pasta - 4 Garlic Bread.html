Garlic Bread

Serves 180Diet Vegetarian
Recipe overview
Par-baked baguettes spread with garlic-parsley margarine and baked.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Baguettes, Parbaked 45 ea —
Garlic 1 kg Pureed
Margarine 4 kg —
Parsley, fresh 1 kg Chop coarse for blending, use leaves & stems

Method

  1. Purée the garlic and blitz briefly with the parsley, then mix into the margarine with plenty of salt.
  2. Cut each baguette lengthways and across into four pieces.
  3. Spread with the garlic butter.
  4. Bake at 220°C until golden and crisp.
