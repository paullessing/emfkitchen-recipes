Arroz con leche

Diet Vegetarian
Recipe overview
Rice pudding cooked in milk with cinnamon, sugar and lemon zest, thickened with cornflour; a soy/oat batch for vegans.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Rice, Long Grain 4.8 kg Wash thoroughly
Milk 24 l —
GF Oat Milk 6 l —
Cinnamon 140 g —
Sugar 4.5 kg —
Lemons 23 ea Rasp for zest to add
Cornflour 800 g —

Method

  1. Wash the long-grain rice thoroughly and cook in the rice cooker.
  2. Warm the milk with cinnamon, sugar and lemon zest.
  3. Combine with the rice; thicken with a cornflour-and-cold-milk slurry.
  4. Make a separate soy/oat-milk batch — label it.
