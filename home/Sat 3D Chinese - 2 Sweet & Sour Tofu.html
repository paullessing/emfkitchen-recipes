Sweet & Sour Tofu

Serves 150Diet Vegan
Recipe overview
Cornflour-dusted baked tofu coated in the sweet & sour sauce.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Tofu 23 kg Cubes

Method

  1. Salt the tofu, dust with cornflour and bake until crisp.
  2. Coat in the sweet & sour sauce and vegetables at service.
