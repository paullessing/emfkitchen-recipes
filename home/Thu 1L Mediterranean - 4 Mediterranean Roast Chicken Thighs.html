Mediterranean Roast Chicken Thighs

Serves 60Vessel ovenDiet Meat
Recipe overview
Skin-on thighs marinated in lemon, garlic, herbs and smoked paprika, then roasted crisp.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Chicken Thighs, skin-on 20 kg —
Lemons, juice of 32 ea juiced
Thyme 160 g —
Oregano 160 g —
Smoked Paprika 160 g —
Olive Oil 1 L —

Method

  1. Make the marinade: lemon juice, garlic, olive oil, thyme, oregano, smoked paprika and salt.
  2. Coat the thighs and marinate overnight in the fridge.
  3. Roast at 200–230°C until crisp and cooked through.
