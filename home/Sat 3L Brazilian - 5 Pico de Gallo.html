Pico de Gallo

Serves 190Diet Vegan
Recipe overview
Fresh salsa of tomato, red onion, jalapeño, garlic and coriander with lime.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Red onions 4 kg Chopped very fine
Tomatoes 6 kg Chopped very fine
Tinned jalapenos (fresh would be awesome!) 3 kg Chopped very fine
Garlic 1 kg —
Coriander Fresh 0.5 kg Chopped fine
Limes 0.5 box Squeezed

Method

  1. Finely chop the tomatoes, red onion, jalapeños and coriander, and combine.
  2. Season to taste with lime juice and a little jalapeño pickle juice.
  3. Keep a separate portion without jalapeños. Keep cold until service.
