Thai Green Chickpea Curry

Serves 180Vessel kipperDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
Green curry with chickpeas and mixed vegetables in coconut milk, seasoned with palm sugar, vegan fish sauce, tamari and lime leaves, finished with Thai basil.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Tinned Chickpeas 32 kg Drained, chickpea water reserved
Green Curry Paste 4.8 kg —
Tamari 0.6 L —
Makrut (Kaffir) Lime Leaves 300 g torn
Palm Sugar 1.5 kg —
Red Onions 9 kg 1inch pieces
Courgette 9 kg 1in cubes, ALL CUBES SAME SIZE
Green Peppers 6 kg about same size as courgettes
Butternut Squash 7.5 kg split, de seed, then peel, cubes
Aubergine 5 kg quartered lengthways
Coconut Milk 25 L —
Fish Sauce, Vegan 1 l For seasoning
Thai Basil 750 g Chopped coarse or chiffonade
Green Chillies 300 g Chopped fine, served on the side

Method

  1. Fry the onions, add green curry paste and fry until aromatic.
  2. Add the vegetables (courgette, pepper, squash, aubergine) and stir to coat.
  3. Add coconut milk and the drained chickpeas; simmer.
  4. Loosen with the reserved chickpea water if it dries out.
  5. Season with palm sugar, vegan fish sauce, tamari and torn lime leaves.
  6. Finish with Thai basil; serve chopped green chillies on the side.
