Onion Rings

Diet Vegan
Recipe overview
Onion rings dipped in a milk-and-flour slurry, coated in panko and deep-fried.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Onions 20 kg Sliced into wide rings
GF Oat Milk 5 kg —
Flour 5 kg —
Paprika 0.3 kg —
Garlic Powder 0.3 kg —
Panko Breadcrumbs 3 kg —

Method

  1. Make a 1:1 slurry of oat milk and flour with paprika and garlic powder. Start on the thin side and thicken as you go.
  2. Dip the onion rings in the slurry, then coat in panko.
  3. Deep-fry in the kipper until golden.
