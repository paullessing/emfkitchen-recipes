Khao Ka Moo (Stewed pork knuckle)

Serves 80Vessel KipperDiet Contains pork
Recipe overview
Thai braised pork knuckle in a spiced soy master-stock with star anise, cinnamon and galangal; served with rice, chilli vinegar and pickled mustard greens.

Method

  1. Sear the pork knuckle on all sides (a bit of colour is fine); put in a big pot/kipper and cover with water.
  2. Add both soy sauces, the oyster sauce and palm sugar; boil ~45 min, skimming off scum.
  3. Dry-toast the cinnamon, star anise, Szechuan and white pepper; add to the pork with the mushrooms, garlic, galangal and coriander.
  4. Boil 2.5–4 hrs until the pork is fall-off-the-bone tender.
  5. Remove the pork and strain the stock.
  6. Debone the pork and remove the skin; reduce the sauce with the bones and skin in it.
  7. Season with dark soy, strain out the bones; reserve the skin to serve separately.
  8. Return the cut pork to the sauce before reheating for service.
  9. Serve with pickled mustard greens on the side.
