Sweet & Sour Chicken

Serves 100Vessel ovenDiet Meat
Recipe overview
Baked or fried chicken thigh coated in the sweet & sour sauce.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Chicken Thighs Diced 15 kg —

Method

  1. Salt the chicken; bake or fry until cooked.
  2. Steam the veg (peppers, carrots, onion, pineapple).
  3. Simmer the sauce and thicken with a starch slurry.
  4. Fold in the chicken and veg; finish with spring onions.
