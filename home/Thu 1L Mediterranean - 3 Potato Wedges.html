Potato Wedges

Serves 260Vessel ovenDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
Skin-on wedges tossed in spiced oil and roasted.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Potatoes 40 kg Wedges, skin on

Method

  1. Mix the spices with oil.
  2. Toss the wedges through and spread on trays.
  3. Salt and pepper on top.
  4. Bake until crisp.
