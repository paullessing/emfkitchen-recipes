Sausages

Serves 260Vessel kipper/hold warmDiet Meat + vegan bat
Recipe overview
Pork sausages roasted in trays; vegan sausages cooked separately and held hot.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Vegan Sausages 20 kg —
Sausages 60 kg —

Method

  1. Roast the pork sausages in trays until browned and cooked through.
  2. Cook the vegan sausages separately (kipper), kept apart from the meat.
  3. Hold hot for service.
