Vegan Vanilla Sheet Cake

Serves 480 slicesVessel 14 GNsDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
One-bowl vegan sponge — soy milk soured with vinegar, mixed with the dry ingredients and oil, baked in trays, then topped with white icing and sprinkles once cold.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Soy Drink 10 l —
Apple Cider Vinegar 400 ml —
Flour, Self Raising 9.2 kg —
Baking Soda 100 g —
Sugar 7.2 kg —
Oil 4 l —
Vanilla Flavouring 1 l —
Icing Sugar 8 kg —
Water 1.2 l —
Cream of Tartar 50 g —
Sprinkles 0.1 kg —

Method

  1. Mix soy drink and cider vinegar; leave 15 min to curdle.
  2. Combine flour, baking soda and caster sugar.
  3. Add the curdled soy, oil and vanilla; mix smooth with no dry pockets.
  4. Pour into lined GN trays, spread level. Bake ~180°C for 20–25 min until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool completely before icing.
  6. Mix icing sugar, water and cream of tartar to a thick, spreadable white glaze; spread over the cold cake.
  7. Scatter sprinkles; leave ~1 hr to set before cutting into squares.
Batch amounts above are already scaled to ~480 slices / 14 GNs (original recipe makes 12).
