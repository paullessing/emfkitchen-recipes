Meat Lasagne

Yield 90p / 6TVessel 4 traysDiet Contains beef
Recipe overview
Beef ragù layered with pasta, béchamel and mozzarella, baked in two stages.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Onions 8 kg —
Garlic 3 kg —
Tinned tomatoes 7.5 kg —
Lasagne sheets (vg, dry) 4.5 kg —
Beef Mince 11 kg —
Carrots 3 kg —
Dried Basil 0.1 kg —
Oregano 75 g —
Balsamic Vinegar 1 l —
Mozzarella, grated or pizza 6 kg —

Method

  1. Fry onions, garlic and herbs until golden.
  2. Add the beef mince and brown.
  3. Deglaze with balsamic; add tomatoes and carrots, and simmer.
  4. Layer: ragù, pasta, béchamel; repeat.
  5. Bake ~220°C for 30–45 min, then cool overnight.
  6. Top with mozzarella and bake ~230°C for 10 min until browned.
