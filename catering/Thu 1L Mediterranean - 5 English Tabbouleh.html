Yield 260Vessel Trays/hot waterDiet Vegetarian
Recipe overview
Couscous salad with lots of parsley, peppers and cucumber, olive oil and lemon; made with and without feta.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Couscous, Dry 6 kg —
Parsley, fresh 4 kg very coarse
Red Peppers 8 kg Diced fine
Cucumber 20 ea remove middle and dice fine
Feta 3 kg —
Olive Oil 3 L liquid
Lemons, juice of 70 ea juice

Method

  1. Hydrate the couscous and fluff.
  2. Fold in parsley, red peppers, cucumber, feta, olive oil and lemon juice.
  3. Season. Make a batch with and a batch without feta.
