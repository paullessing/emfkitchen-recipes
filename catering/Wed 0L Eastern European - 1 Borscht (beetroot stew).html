Borscht (beetroot stew)

Yield 80Vessel TopfDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
Beetroot and vegetable stew with red lentils, built by frying the vegetables in stages, simmered in stock and finished with dill; served with sour cream.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Beetroot 7.5 kg peeled and cubed, can be grated if time is short
Carrots 6 kg peeled and cubed, can be grated if time is short
Potatoes 7.5 kg peeled and cubed, no grating
Celeriac 2 ea peeled and cubed
Dill, Fresh 0.5 kg —
Red lentils 2.5 kg wash before use
Tinned Tomatoes 12.5 kg —
Leeks 6 kg washed and cut into strips
White cabbage 3 kg shred through machine med setting
Onions 7.5 kg medium cubes
Apple Cider Vinegar 0.8 L —
Stock 15 L —
Smoked Paprika 120 g —
Paprika, Hot 70 g —
Sour cream 3 kg —
Sour Cream, Vegan 1 kg —

Method

  1. Wash the red lentils until the water runs clear.
  2. Fry carrots and celeriac until soft and browned.
  3. Add onions, leeks and paprika (smoked + hot); fry until golden.
  4. Add cabbage and beetroot; fry to soften.
  5. Add stock and potatoes.
  6. Simmer until potatoes are almost done.
  7. Add lentils and dill; simmer to thicken.
  8. Serve with sour cream (dairy + vegan) on the side.
