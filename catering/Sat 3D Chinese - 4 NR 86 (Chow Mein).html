NR 86 (Chow Mein)

Yield 100Vessel kipperDiet Vegetarian
Recipe overview
Stir-fried egg noodles with vegetables in a soy and mushroom stir-fry sauce.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Egg Noodles, dry 8 kg —
Mushroom Stir Fry Sauce 2.4 L —
Soy Sauce, Dark 1.2 L —
Soy Sauce 1.2 L All sauces mixed together
Carrots 5 kg grated with machine
Cabbage 3 kg medium machine cutting disc
Onions 8 kg medium machine cutting disc
Green Peppers 6 kg de seeded, no white, thin strips
Spring Onions 2 kg Sliced long ways but fast ways
Toasted Sesame Oil 0.5 l —
MSG 50 g —
Garlic 0.5 kg —
Mushrooms 7 kg quartered

Method

  1. Fry onions with dried chilli over high heat.
  2. Add the veg (carrot, cabbage, pepper, mushroom) and keep it crisp.
  3. Add the cooked noodles and sesame oil; toss.
  4. Add the soy sauces and stir-fry sauce; finish with spring onions.
