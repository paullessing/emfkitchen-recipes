Cauliflower Cheese

Yield 260Vessel oven, hold hotDiet Vegetarian
Recipe overview
Cauliflower and broccoli baked in cheese sauce; a separate gluten-free batch uses GF flour, oat milk and engevita.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Cauliflower 40 ea Cut into florets, peel stems and cut into rounds
Broccoli 16 kg Cut into florets, peel stems and cut into rounds
Grated Cheese 8 kg —
Flour 4 kg —
Gluten Free Flour 1000 g —
Milk, UHT 30 L —
GF Oat Milk 10 L —
Margarine 8 kg —
White pepper 100 g —
Engevita 400 g —

Method

  1. Steam cauliflower and broccoli until just tender.
  2. Melt margarine and stir in flour to make a roux.
  3. Add UHT milk, stirring constantly until boiled; fold in cheese, salt and white pepper.
  4. Make a GF batch in a separate pan: GF flour + oat milk + engevita.
  5. Stir the sauce through the veg in trays; top with remaining cheese.
  6. Bake until golden.
