Chickpea Pepper Stew

Yield 180Vessel saucepanDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
Onions fried with tomato purée, then tinned tomatoes and a mix of roasted vegetables simmered down and spiced. Drained chickpeas stirred in at the end so they stay whole.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Tinned Chickpeas 32 kg drained
Onions 12 kg chopped
Red Peppers 5 kg 1in squares
Green Peppers 5 kg 1in squares
Tinned Tomatoes 12 kg —
Tomato Puree 2 kg —
Courgette 5 kg 1in cubes
White Cabbage 8 kg 1in squares
Savoy Cabbage 9 ea 1in squares
Carrots 8 kg 1in cubes
Swede 5 kg 1in cubes
Garam Masala 100 g —
Cumin 100 g —
Paprika 100 g —

Method

  1. Roast peppers, courgette, cabbage, carrot and swede until edges caramelise (if oven space).
  2. Fry onions in oil until soft and translucent.
  3. Add tomato purée and cook out 1–2 min until darkened.
  4. Add tinned tomatoes and all the veg; season with garam masala, cumin and paprika.
  5. Simmer until veg is just tender — a knife meets slight resistance.
  6. Stir in drained chickpeas and simmer to heat through. Taste and adjust seasoning.
