Leftover Gumbo (vg)

Diet Vegan
Recipe overview
Vegan gumbo from leftover vegetables and beans in a roux-thickened vegetable stock, over rice.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Uses leftovers / see notes

Method

  1. Make an oil-based roux cooked to a deep brown.
  2. Add onion, pepper and celery if available; cook down.
  3. Add leftover vegetables and beans.
  4. Add vegetable stock and simmer to thicken.
  5. Season; serve over rice.
Flexible — built from whatever is left. Keep separate from the meat gumbo.
