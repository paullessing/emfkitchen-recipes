Brazilian Cottage Pie

Yield 110Vessel 9TDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
Vegetable and soy-protein filling under a butternut-and-potato mash, topped with breadcrumbs and baked. Also called <i>Vegetable Escondidinho</i>.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Butternut Squash 10 kg Roasted, Mashed
Potatoes 12 kg Cooked, Mashed
Vegetable Stock 3 l —
Oil 700 ml —
Salt 120 g —
Pepper 30 g —
Breadcrumbs (Leftover) 2 kg —
Onions 4 kg Diced 1 inch
Garlic 300 g —
Carrots 5 kg Diced 1 inch
Red Peppers 5 kg Diced 1 inch
Courgette 4 kg Diced 1 inch
Mushrooms 5 kg Sliced
Frozen Sweetcorn 3 kg —
Tinned Tomatoes 5 kg —
Tomato Puree 1 kg —
Soy Protein 3 kg —
Vegetable Stock 3 l —
Lime Juice, Bottle 0.5 l —
Parsley, fresh 600 g —
Cumin 200 g —
Smoked Paprika 200 g —
Ground Coriander 150 g —
Oregano 80 g —
Pepper 50 g —

Method

  1. Roast the squash and boil the potatoes; mash together with stock and season well.
  2. Sweat and cook down the filling: onion, garlic, carrot, pepper, courgette, mushroom, sweetcorn, soy protein, tomato and spices. Finish with lime and parsley.
  3. Top the filling with the mash, then breadcrumbs.
  4. Bake until golden.
