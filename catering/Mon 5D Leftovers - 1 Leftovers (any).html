Method

  1. Sort the leftovers and keep veg/vegan separate from meat.
  2. Reheat thoroughly to piping hot throughout, stirring; do not reheat any item more than once.
  3. Refresh salads and cold sides; keep cold items cold.
  4. Serve, and label everything by diet.
