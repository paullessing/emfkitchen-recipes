Sweet & Sour Sauce

Yield 250Diet Vegan
Recipe overview
Takeaway-style sweet & sour: pineapple juice, brown sugar, ketchup, soy and rice vinegar simmered and thickened with starch, with pineapple and vegetables through it.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Onions 20 kg big cubes
Red Peppers 20 kg 1inch squares
Carrots 20 kg 1inch cubes
Pineapple 20 kg peeled, cubed (1inch)
Spring Onions 7.5 kg chopped coarse
Pineapple Juice 15 L —
Sugar, light brown soft 7.5 kg —
Ketchup 6 kg —
Soy Sauce 2.5 L —
Rice Wine Vinegar 3.75 L —
Potato Starch 1.5 kg —

Method

  1. Steam the vegetables (peppers, carrots, onion).
  2. Put everything except the starch in a pot — pineapple juice, brown sugar, ketchup, soy and rice wine vinegar — and bring to a simmer, whisking.
  3. Make a potato-starch (or cornflour) slurry with water; whisk in and cook until thickened and glossy.
  4. Fold the vegetables and pineapple through; finish with spring onions.
