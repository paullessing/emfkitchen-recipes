Steamed Veg

Yield 260Vessel OvenDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
Peas and sliced carrots, steamed briefly to keep them bright.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Frozen Peas 10 kg —
Carrots 10 kg —

Method

  1. Cut the carrots small.
  2. Steam carrots and peas ~3 minutes, checking often so they stay bright and just tender.
