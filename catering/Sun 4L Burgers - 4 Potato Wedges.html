Potato Wedges

Diet Vegan
Recipe overview
Skin-on wedges in smoked-paprika oil, roasted.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Potatoes 70 kg —
Smoked Paprika 0.5 kg —
Olive Oil 1 l —

Method

  1. Toss the potatoes in oil and smoked paprika.
  2. Roast until crisp.
