Asian Cucumber Salad

Diet Vegan
Recipe overview
Salted cucumber in a garlic-chilli-sesame dressing with nori and sriracha.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Cucumber 50 ea Machine Slice
Toasted Sesame Oil 1 L —
Chilli Flakes 100 g —
Rice Wine Vinegar 200 ml —
Salt 300 g —
Sugar, Brown 250 g —
Garlic 3 ea —
Sesame Seeds 350 g Dry toast in pan
Nori 65 g —
Sriracha Sauce 150 ml —
Soy Sauce 0.5 l —

Method

  1. Slice the cucumbers as fine as possible; salt and sugar, then drain.
  2. Mince the garlic fine (or use roast purée).
  3. Toast the sesame seeds; cut nori into 1cm flakes.
  4. Combine garlic, chilli flakes, nori, sriracha, vinegar and soy.
  5. Scatter the hot toasted seeds over the garlic.
  6. Heat the sesame oil and pour over to bloom the aromatics.
  7. Toss the cucumbers through; taste and adjust.
