Tomato Sauce

Yield 180Diet Vegan
Recipe overview
Slow-cooked tomato sauce: onions and herbs fried off, deglazed with balsamic, then tinned tomatoes reduced with carrots that are puréed back in to sweeten and thicken.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Tinned Tomatoes 70 kg —
Onions 25 kg medium cubes
Oil 3 l —
Basil, Fresh 1 kg Chopped or Chiffonade
Basil, Dried 0.1 kg —
Oregano 0.1 kg —
Provence Herbs 0.1 kg —
Balsamic Vinegar 3 l —
Bay Leaves 0.2 kg —
Carrots 15 kg peeled, chopped very large

Method

  1. Fry onions with fresh & dried basil, oregano, Provence herbs and bay until lightly coloured.
  2. Deglaze with balsamic vinegar.
  3. Add the tinned tomatoes.
  4. Add the large-cut carrots and reduce.
  5. Optional: let it cool.
  6. Remove the carrots, purée them, and stir back in.
  7. Reheat and simmer to a thick sauce; season.

Beef & Ale Stew

Yield 60Vessel KipperDiet Contains: beef, gluten (flour
Recipe overview
Beef is browned off, vegetables sweated in the same pan. Deglaze with ale, simmer with stock, and thicken with Bisto at the end.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep

Pepper, Cracked 100 g —
Ale 6 l —
Flour 1.5 kg —
Onions 10 kg medium cubes

Swede 5 kg peeled, machine cubes

Method

Vegan Vanilla Sheet Cake

Yield 480 slicesVessel 14 GNsDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
One-bowl vegan sponge — soy milk soured with vinegar, mixed with the dry ingredients and oil, baked in trays, then topped with white icing and sprinkles once cold.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Soy Drink 10 l —
Apple Cider Vinegar 400 ml —
Flour, Self Raising 9.2 kg —
Baking Soda 100 g —
Sugar 7.2 kg —
Oil 4 l —
Vanilla Flavouring 1 l —
Icing Sugar 8 kg —
Water 1.2 l —
Cream of Tartar 50 g —
Sprinkles 0.1 kg —

Method

  1. Mix soy drink and cider vinegar; leave 15 min to curdle.
  2. Combine flour, baking soda and caster sugar.
  3. Add the curdled soy, oil and vanilla; mix smooth with no dry pockets.

Khao Ka Moo (Stewed pork knuckle)

Yield 80Vessel KipperDiet Contains pork
Recipe overview
Thai braised pork knuckle in a spiced soy master-stock with star anise, cinnamon and galangal; served with rice, chilli vinegar and pickled mustard greens.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Pork knuckle 24 kg —
Light Soy Sauce 1280 ml —
Oyster Sauce 1280 ml —
Dark Soy Sauce 640 ml —
Palm Sugar 1.6 kg —
Fresh Coriander 0 kg —
Garlic 500 g —
Garlic cloves 500 g —
Cinnamon Sticks 160 g —
Star Anise 128 g —
Szechuan Peppercorns 80 g —
White Pepper 32 g —
Galangal 1.6 kg —
Shiitake Mushrooms 640 g —
Pickled Mustard Greens 12 bags —

Method

  1. Sear the pork knuckle on all sides (a bit of colour is fine); put in a big pot/kipper and cover with water.
  2. Add both soy sauces, the oyster sauce and palm sugar; boil ~45 min, skimming off scum.
  3. Dry-toast the cinnamon, star anise, Szechuan and white pepper; add to the pork with the mushrooms, garlic, galangal and coriander.
  4. Boil 2.5–4 hrs until the pork is fall-off-the-bone tender.
  5. Remove the pork and strain the stock.
  6. Debone the pork and remove the skin; reduce the sauce with the bones and skin in it.
  7. Season with dark soy, strain out the bones; reserve the skin to serve separately.
  8. Return the cut pork to the sauce before reheating for service.
  9. Serve with pickled mustard greens on the side.

Tofu Feijoada

Yield 120Vessel topfDiet Vegan
Recipe overview
Vegan feijoada: spiced fried jackfruit crushed for a pulled texture, in a smoky black-bean stew thickened by mashing some of the beans.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Jackfruit, tinned 25 kg —
Black Beans 40 kg Drained, 1/3-1/2 mashed
Onions 13 kg Chopped large
Garlic 1 kg —
Smoked Paprika 0.2 kg —
Cumin 0.1 kg —
Bay Leaves 0.1 kg —
Stock, vegetable, homemade 20 L —
Red Peppers 12 kg —
Liquid smoke 0.5 L —
Lime Juice, Bottle 1.5 L —

Method

  1. Dust the jackfruit with cumin and paprika and fry in plenty of oil until browned; set aside.
  2. Fry the onions until soft; add garlic and smoked paprika.
  3. Add the peppers and fry.
  4. Return the jackfruit and crush with a potato masher to get a fibrous, pulled texture.
  5. Add bay leaves and stock; simmer to meld.
  6. Drain the black beans (keep the liquid); mash about a third, add all the beans and simmer to thicken.
  7. Season with liquid smoke, salt and lime; loosen with the bean liquid if needed.
