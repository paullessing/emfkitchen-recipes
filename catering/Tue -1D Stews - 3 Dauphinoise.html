Dauphinoise

Vessel ovenDiet Vegetarian
Recipe overview
Sliced potatoes baked in seasoned garlic cream, covered with foil, until tender, then held hot.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Potatoes 45 kg peeled, machine slices, large
Cream, Double 15 l —
White Pepper 45 g —
Salt 315 g —
Garlic 3 kg roasted and pureed with oil. make a LOT

Method

  1. Recipe fills 2 trays — halve everything and make twice.
  2. Mix cream, roasted garlic purée, salt and white pepper in the GN tray.
  3. Submerge sliced potatoes in the seasoned cream.
  4. Cover with a double layer of foil, well sealed.
  5. Bake at 120–180°C, covered, at least 3 hours, until a knife goes through with no resistance.
  6. Hold hot until service.
