Stuffed Peppers

Diet Vegan
Recipe overview
Peppers halved and stuffed with a spiced rice-and-vegetable filling, then baked.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Red Peppers 5 kg Halved, deseeded (shells)
Green Peppers 5 kg Halved, deseeded (shells)
Courgette 5 kg Diced (filling)
Onions 7 kg Diced (filling)
Rice existing Leftover from previous day
Smoked Paprika 0.2 kg —
Cumin 0.2 kg —
Olive Oil 0.5 L —
Grated Cheese 2 kg —
Mozzarella, vegan 1 kg —

Method

  1. Halve and deseed the peppers to make shells.
  2. Make the filling: fry onion and courgette with cumin and smoked paprika, then mix through the (leftover) rice.
  3. Stuff the pepper halves and top with cheese.
  4. Bake until the peppers are tender.
