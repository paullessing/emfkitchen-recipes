Carrot Salad

Diet Vegan
Recipe overview
Finely grated carrot dressed with lemon, oil and vinegar, finished with sesame and parsley; rested overnight.

Ingredients & Prep

Ingredient Amount Prep
Carrots 30 kg peeled
Lemon Juice 2.5 l —
White wine vinegar 2.5 l —
Sesame Seeds 300 g —
Olive Oil 2.5 l —
Fresh Parsley 2 kg —

Method

  1. Grate the carrots finely (machine).
  2. Dress with lemon juice, olive oil and white wine vinegar.
  3. Scrunch/knead to soften the carrot.
  4. Stir through sesame seeds and parsley.
  5. Rest overnight before serving.
